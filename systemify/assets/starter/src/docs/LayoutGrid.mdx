# Layout grid

One grid, three modes. Demo values: replace them with the audit's.

| Mode | From | Columns | Gutter | Page margin | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| small screens | 0 | 4 | 16px | 24px | fluid |
| md | 800px | 8 | 24px | 40px | fluid |
| lg | 1280px | 12 | 32px | 56px | max 1168px (margins grow beyond) |

The breakpoints come from the audit: the widths where the source layout changes.

**In code**: `Grid` and `Col`. A Col says how many columns it spans per mode:

```tsx
<Grid>
  <Col md={4} lg={5}>image</Col>   {/* 5 of 12 from lg, 4 of 8 from md, full by default */}
  <Col md={4} lg={7}>text</Col>
</Grid>
```

Example layouts on the grid:

| Pattern | lg | md | small screens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Media + text | 5 + 7 | 4 + 4 | stacked, media first |
| Two cards | 6 + 6 | 4 + 4 | stacked |
| Three cards | 4 + 4 + 4 | stacked | stacked |
